architecture

The percentages in these tiers come from simulation and the link figure from a host benchmark, not from boards in a room. Board prices are for ESP32-S3 devkits; the gateway is any small Linux machine. Consumer laptops expose RSSI only, which is enough for crude motion but not for breathing.

built for a campus without cameras

Each use maps to a tier, and each tier states what it cannot do. T0 is built. The others are research or come from existing campus IT.

Lights and AC off in empty rooms

T0

Room presence, including still people. The highest value at the lowest risk.

Lab lone-worker safety

T0

No motion for N minutes in a lab raises an advisory alert to the warden. It does not replace procedures.

Hostel corridors at night

T0

Motion only, with no identity and no camera where one would never be acceptable.

Classroom headcount

T1 · research

Counts in coarse bins, trained per room. It cannot take attendance because it does not know who anyone is.

Library free seats

T2 · research

Zone level at best. Position needs a mesh, and the first simulation says it is hard.

Evacuation sweep

T4 + T0

Building counts from existing access-point analytics plus per-room occupancy. At the building level it counts devices, not people.
